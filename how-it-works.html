Straight answers

Questions owners ask us first

Will you take money from my bank account?

No. Customers pay your business, into your account. BrandSure cannot touch your collections.

Do you take shares in my company?

No. No shares. No ownership. Your company stays 100% yours.

Do I have to pay the ad budget?

It depends which way you work with us. On the monthly marketing plans you pay your own ad spend directly. Under the Growth Partnership, selected clients may receive BrandSure-funded Google and Meta advertising.

Who handles the factory, workers and delivery?

You and your team. That side of the business stays fully with you.

What does BrandSure charge?

There are two routes. The monthly marketing plans are priced on the Prices page — ₹45,000 and ₹75,000 a month, with ad spend paid directly by you. For selected manufacturers there is a longer-term Growth Partnership, where our commercial structure is aligned with the growth of the business. Partnership commercials are explained during the business review, using your own numbers: your product cost, your selling price, expected sales, what you keep and what BrandSure receives.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

If we don't believe the plan can work for your business, we will tell you that before starting — and step back ourselves.
