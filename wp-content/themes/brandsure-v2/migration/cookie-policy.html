This Cookie Policy explains how https://brandsure.in, operated by BrandSure Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies let a site remember your actions and preferences, and let its owner understand how the site is being used. Similar technologies — pixels, tags, local storage and software development kits — do comparable things, and we use “cookies” in this policy to cover all of them.

Categories of cookies used on this website

Strictly necessary

These are needed for the website to work — for example to keep the site secure, to balance load, or to remember a choice you have made about cookies. The website cannot be provided properly without them.

Analytics and performance

The website records a small number of events — which pages are opened, whether a video is started, and whether the WhatsApp button is used — so we can see where the site is unhelpful and fix it. These events are recorded against a random identifier, not against your name, and they are sent to BrandSure’s own systems rather than to an advertising network.

The one cookie we set

bs_vid — a first-party cookie containing ten random characters. It is created the first time you open the website and lasts 180 days. Its only purpose is to let us join up the pages you looked at with the WhatsApp conversation you start afterwards, so nobody asks you the same question twice. It contains nothing about you, it is not used for advertising, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Third parties that may set cookies

BrandSure does not place advertising or analytics cookies from third parties on this website. Two third parties are involved when you choose to use them:

  • YouTube — a video player is loaded only if you tap play on a video. YouTube may then set its own cookies. If you do not tap play, nothing is loaded from YouTube.
  • WhatsApp — the WhatsApp buttons are ordinary links. They set no cookie here; WhatsApp’s own terms apply once you open a conversation.

Our hosting and security providers may set strictly necessary cookies. If BrandSure later adds advertising or analytics tools to this website, this policy will be updated before they are switched on.

WhatsApp links

Buttons on this website that open WhatsApp take you to a service operated by WhatsApp. Once you leave this website, WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy terms apply to that interaction.

How to control cookies

  • In your browser — every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and block third-party cookies specifically. Look under Settings, then Privacy.
  • Google advertising — you can adjust personalised advertising in your Google account ad settings.
  • Meta advertising — you can adjust ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.
  • Device settings — mobile operating systems offer controls that limit ad tracking.

Blocking cookies in the strictly necessary category may stop parts of the website from working correctly. Blocking analytics or advertising cookies does not stop you using the website.
