Straight answers

The questions owners actually ask us.

Another agency quoted me ₹25,000 a month. Why is yours higher?

Because you are buying different things. A ₹25,000 retainer usually buys campaign management, shared across a dozen unrelated industries. Agency Plan at ₹45,000 buys a team that works only on Indian furniture and sofa businesses, so the ads speak to a sofa buyer, not to a generic “home decor” audience. Digital Partner at ₹75,000 buys the part where most furniture businesses are quietly losing money: somebody calling every enquiry quickly, chasing it on WhatsApp and booking the visit. Compare that against a telecaller, a marketing executive, a designer and a CRM on your own payroll.

What if it does not work in three months?

Agency Plan and Digital Partner carry a three-month minimum and then run month to month. Three months is the shortest honest window to launch, collect real data and fix what the data shows. One month is guessing. But you are not waiting three months to find out: you get a review on real numbers at the end of month one, and again every month after.

Will you work with my competitor in the same city?

Territory and selling locations are agreed in writing before any work starts. Digital Partner includes one city, with each additional city at ₹20,000 a month. Ask us about your specific city and product category before you sign, and we will tell you exactly what we can commit to.

Do I need a website before we start?

No. Google and Meta campaigns run to a landing page and to WhatsApp, so you can start without one. A website is an add-on at ₹90,000 whenever you want it. Do not let a missing website delay the enquiries.

My son already runs our Instagram. Where does that fit?

It stays with him. Organic social is the brand’s voice and it is better in the family’s hands. What we run is paid Google and Meta: the part that needs daily budget decisions, audience testing and a person watching the cost per enquiry. The two work better side by side than merged.

Who owns the leads and the CRM data?

You do. The enquiries are your customers, the CRM records are your records, and you have visibility into leads, calls and chats once setup is complete. If we stop working together, the data does not walk out with us.

What do you need from me?

Three things. Access to your ad accounts and WhatsApp number. Your product and price list, so the calling desk quotes nothing you have not approved. And a steady flow of fresh photos and videos from the showroom, factory, installations and finished work. Ads built on your real work consistently beat ads built on stock images, and this is the one input we cannot produce for you.

Can I start small and move up later?

Yes, and most owners should. Start on Agency Plan, see whether the enquiries arrive, and move to Digital Partner when the bottleneck shifts to follow-up, which it usually does. Those two are the only growth marketing plans; the Growth Partnership is a separate conversation.

Is there a way to work with BrandSure beyond marketing?

Yes. Selected furniture manufacturers work with BrandSure under a longer-term Growth Partnership covering growth, product, sales systems and business development. It is a different business relationship rather than a more expensive marketing plan, so it is not sold as an upgrade to the two plans above.

It is qualification-led: we look at the business first and the partnership structure is explained after that review. If you want to know whether your business is suitable, ask us.
