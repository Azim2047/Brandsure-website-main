What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies let a site remember your actions and preferences, and let its owner understand how the site is being used. Similar technologies — pixels, tags, local storage and software development kits — do comparable things, and we use “cookies” in this policy to cover all of them.

Categories of cookies used on this website

Strictly necessary

These are needed for the website to work — for example to keep the site secure, to balance load, or to remember a choice you have made about cookies. The website cannot be provided properly without them.

Analytics and performance

These help us understand how visitors use the website in aggregate — which pages are viewed, how long people stay, where they leave, and which links they click. We use this to fix problems and improve the site. Google Analytics is the main service used in this category.

Advertising and remarketing

These are set by advertising platforms so that campaigns can be measured and so that BrandSure advertising can be shown to people who have previously visited this website. They typically include cookies and pixels associated with Google Ads and with Meta advertising for Facebook and Instagram. These technologies may also be used to build audiences for future advertising.

Third parties that may set cookies

  • Google — Google Analytics, and Google Ads conversion measurement and remarketing.
  • Meta Platforms — the Meta pixel and related tools for Facebook and Instagram advertising and measurement.
  • Other measurement or session-analysis providers used from time to time to diagnose usability problems.
  • Our hosting and security providers — for cookies in the strictly necessary category.

These third parties set and read their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control the cookies they set, and the list above may change as the tools we use change.

WhatsApp links

Buttons on this website that open WhatsApp take you to a service operated by WhatsApp. Once you leave this website, WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy terms apply to that interaction.

How to control cookies

  • In your browser — every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and block third-party cookies specifically. Look under Settings, then Privacy.
  • Google advertising — you can adjust personalised advertising in your Google account ad settings.
  • Meta advertising — you can adjust ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.
  • Device settings — mobile operating systems offer controls that limit ad tracking.

Blocking cookies in the strictly necessary category may stop parts of the website from working correctly. Blocking analytics or advertising cookies does not stop you using the website.
