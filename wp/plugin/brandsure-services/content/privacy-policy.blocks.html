This Privacy Policy explains how BrandSure Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. (“BrandSure”, “we”, “us”) handles information collected through https://brandsure.in and through the enquiry channels linked from it. BrandSure is a company registered in India (CIN U73100KA2025PTC199105) and works with furniture manufacturers and showroom-led furniture businesses.

We handle personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, to the extent those provisions are in force and apply to us.

Information we collect

The information we hold falls into a small number of categories.

Information you give us

  • Your name, business name, phone number and email address when you make an enquiry.
  • The city or location of your business, and details of what you manufacture or sell, where you choose to share them.
  • The content of messages you send us, including messages sent over WhatsApp, email or a form on this website.
  • Any business information you share with us voluntarily during a conversation or an engagement.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and screen size.
  • How you reached the website, including the referring website, search terms where these are made available to us, and campaign parameters in the link you clicked.
  • How you use the website — pages viewed, time on page, scrolling, clicks and similar interaction events.

We do not ask for and do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, bank details, government identifiers or other sensitive personal data through this website.

How we use this information

  • To respond to your enquiry and to continue the conversation you started.
  • To understand whether we may be able to help your business, and to prepare for a discussion with you.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To measure which marketing activity brings relevant enquiries, and to plan future activity.
  • To keep records required for our own business, tax and compliance purposes.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your enquiry details with other furniture businesses.

Analytics, advertising and tracking technologies

This website uses, or may use, third-party services that set cookies or use similar technologies. These typically include:

  • Google Analytics — to understand how the website is used in aggregate.
  • Google Ads — including conversion measurement and remarketing, so our advertising can be measured and shown to people who have previously visited the website.
  • Meta advertising tools — the Meta pixel and related technologies used for Facebook and Instagram advertising, measurement and remarketing.
  • Other measurement or session-analysis tools used from time to time to diagnose usability problems.

These services are operated by the respective third parties, who process the data they collect under their own privacy terms. Where remarketing is used, you may see BrandSure advertising on other websites or platforms after visiting this site. You can usually limit this through your Google and Meta ad settings, and through your browser's cookie controls. Our Cookie Policy explains the categories in more detail.

WhatsApp and other messaging

Several buttons on this website open a WhatsApp conversation with a message already written into it. If you send that message, your phone number and the message content reach us through WhatsApp, and the exchange is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. We keep such conversations for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and to maintain our business records.

Sharing and disclosure

We may share information in a limited set of circumstances:

  • With service providers who operate parts of our infrastructure — for example website hosting, email, analytics and advertising platforms — who process data on our instructions.
  • With our professional advisers where necessary.
  • Where we are required to do so by law, or by a competent authority, court or regulator.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect the rights and safety of any person.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to satisfy ourselves that the data is handled appropriately.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and conversation records for as long as needed to deal with the matter and for a reasonable period afterwards, and for as long as we are required to keep records for legal, tax or accounting purposes. Analytics data is retained according to the settings of the platform concerned.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to the personal data we hold about you, to correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete, or to erase it where there is no continuing need or legal requirement for us to keep it. You may also withdraw a consent you previously gave, and you may ask us to stop sending you marketing messages.

To make a request, write to us using the contact details below. We may need to ask you for information to confirm your identity before we act on a request. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a request or a complaint, you may escalate it to the relevant authority under applicable Indian law.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No website or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot describe any system as absolutely safe, and you share information with us on that understanding.
